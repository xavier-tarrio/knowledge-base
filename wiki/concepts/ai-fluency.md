# AI Fluency

## What it is
The ability to collaborate with AI systems in ways that are **effective, efficient, ethical, and safe**. Not a fixed technical skill set, but a durable framework for human-AI interaction that stays relevant as models evolve. Developed by Rick Dakan and Joseph Feller as an academic framework, adopted and taught by Anthropic Academy.

## Why it matters (for this domain)
AI Fluency is the meta-skill underneath every other skill in this wiki. Prompt engineering is one component (Description). Knowing when to use agentic AI vs. a simple chat is another (Delegation). Building evals is a third (Diligence). Having a single framework that names and connects these is more useful than learning each skill in isolation — especially when teaching others.

Together, the 4Ds tell you *how to think* about an AI interaction; the three modes tell you *what kind of relationship* you're establishing with the AI on that task.

## Practical notes
- AI Fluency is intentionally model-agnostic — it applies to any LLM, not just Claude
- The framework was developed in an academic context (supported by Ireland's Higher Education Authority); it prioritizes conceptual clarity over product-specific tips
- "Fluency" is an analogy to language learning: you don't master a language by memorizing phrases, you internalize grammar. Same principle applies here.

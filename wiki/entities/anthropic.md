# Anthropic

## What it is
AI safety company and creator of Claude. Founded 2021 by former OpenAI researchers Dario Amodei, Daniela Amodei, and others. Headquarters in San Francisco. Primary mission: responsible development and maintenance of advanced AI for the long-term benefit of humanity.

## Products and surfaces
- **Claude** — the underlying intelligence (models: Haiku, Sonnet, Opus)
- **Claude.ai** — primary web/mobile interface
- **Claude Desktop** — native desktop app (Chat, Cowork, Code modes)
- **Claude Code** — agentic coding CLI for terminal/IDE
- **Anthropic API** — programmatic access for developers
- **Claude for Chrome/Excel/Slack** — embedded integrations
- **Anthropic Academy** — official learning platform

## Design philosophy
Anthropic emphasizes Constitutional AI — training models to be helpful, harmless, and honest. This explains Claude's behavioral defaults (careful about harmful content, proactive about flagging uncertainty) and feature design (permissions are scoped and revocable, high-risk browser actions require confirmation).

## Relevance to this domain
Anthropic is the primary toolchain vendor for this knowledge base. Understanding Anthropic's product roadmap and design philosophy predicts where Claude's capabilities are heading — relevant for course building and for identifying which capabilities to build businesses around.

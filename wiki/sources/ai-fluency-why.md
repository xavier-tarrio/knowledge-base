# Anthropic Academy: Why Do We Need AI Fluency?

## Summary
Second lesson of the AI Fluency Framework Foundations course. Argues for AI Fluency as a combination of practical skills, knowledge, insights, and values. Introduces a three-tier model of AI collaboration: Automation, Augmentation, and Agency — each representing a progressively deeper mode of working with AI.

## Key takeaways
- AI Fluency combines skills, knowledge, insights, and values — not just technical ability
- Three collaboration modes: Automation (AI executes instructions), Augmentation (human + AI as co-thinking partners), Agency (human configures AI to work independently)
- Agency mode is the highest tier — you establish the AI's knowledge and behavior patterns rather than giving task-by-task instructions
- These three modes are not stages to progress through; they co-exist and the right one depends on the task

# AI Fluency: A Closer Look at Discernment

## Summary
Deep dive into Discernment — the third 4D competency. Mirrors Description with three evaluation types: Product Discernment (output quality), Process Discernment (reasoning quality), and Performance Discernment (collaboration quality). Positions Discernment as the essential counterpart to Description.

## Key takeaways
- Discernment mirrors Description: Product, Process, and Performance Discernment
- Product Discernment: accuracy, appropriateness, coherence, relevance of outputs
- Process Discernment: logical errors, attention gaps, inappropriate reasoning in how the AI got there
- Performance Discernment: whether the AI's communication style served the collaboration
- Description and Discernment form a continuous feedback loop — not separate activities
- Domain expertise amplifies Discernment: you can only catch errors in areas you know

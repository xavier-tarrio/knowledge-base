# AI Fluency: A Closer Look at Delegation

## Summary
Deep dive into Delegation — the first 4D competency. Introduces three sub-components: Problem Awareness (understanding your goals), Platform Awareness (knowing what AI can do), and Task Delegation (strategically dividing work). Emphasises that effective delegation requires both domain expertise and AI understanding.

## Key takeaways
- Delegation has three sub-components: Problem Awareness, Platform Awareness, Task Delegation
- Problem Awareness: clearly understand your goal before involving AI at all
- Platform Awareness: know the capabilities and limitations of the specific AI system you're using
- Task Delegation: distribute work strategically to leverage each party's strengths
- Effective delegation requires domain expertise — you can't delegate what you don't understand
- The goal is the most effective partnership, not maximum automation

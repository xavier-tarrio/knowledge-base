# AI Fluency: Conclusion

## Summary
Course synthesis lesson. Recaps the full AI Fluency framework, emphasises that fluency develops through intentional practice rather than overnight mastery, and closes with the framing that AI systems are only as useful and safe as we enable them to be.

## Key takeaways
- AI Fluency develops through practice — not a destination but a continuous skill
- Each 4D contributes a distinct layer: Delegation (judgment), Description (communication), Discernment (evaluation), Diligence (responsibility)
- The framework is designed to remain relevant as AI evolves — competencies are model-agnostic
- Most powerful outcomes emerge when humans and AI build on each other's strengths
- Suggests building a Personal AI Policy as a practical Diligence exercise

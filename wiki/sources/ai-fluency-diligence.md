# AI Fluency: A Closer Look at Diligence

## Summary
Deep dive into Diligence — the fourth 4D competency covering ethical and responsible AI use. Three sub-components: Creation Diligence (thoughtful choice of AI system and inputs), Transparency Diligence (being open about AI's role), and Deployment Diligence (owning the final output). Introduces the concept of a Diligence Statement.

## Key takeaways
- Diligence has three sub-components: Creation, Transparency, Deployment
- Creation Diligence: which AI to use, what data to share, privacy and security considerations
- Transparency Diligence: being honest with audiences about AI's role in your work
- Deployment Diligence: taking full responsibility for verifying and vouching for AI-assisted outputs
- Context determines disclosure expectations: personal vs. academic vs. professional are different
- A Diligence Statement is a formal declaration of AI's role and your responsibility for the output

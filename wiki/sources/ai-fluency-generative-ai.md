# AI Fluency: Generative AI Fundamentals

## Summary
Technical foundations lesson of the AI Fluency course. Explains how LLMs like Claude actually work — from transformer architecture to training stages — and why this technical grounding matters for effective delegation and discernment.

## Key takeaways
- Generative AI creates new content rather than classifying or retrieving existing data
- Three developments made modern LLMs possible: transformer architecture, vast training data, and dramatic compute increases
- LLMs learn in two stages: pre-training (pattern recognition across billions of examples) and fine-tuning (instruction following and helpful responses)
- Context windows and emergent capabilities are key concepts for understanding AI behavior

# Anthropic Academy: Introduction to AI Fluency

## Summary
Introductory lesson of the "AI Fluency Framework Foundations" course by Anthropic Academy. Defines AI Fluency as the ability to collaborate with AI effectively, efficiently, ethically, and safely. Introduces the course structure built around four core competencies called the 4Ds, positioning the course as a framework for lasting collaboration skills rather than temporary tips.

## Key takeaways
- AI Fluency is about human-AI collaboration, not understanding AI as a technology
- The 4D competencies (Delegation, Description, Discernment, Diligence) are the structural backbone of the course
- The goal is skills that stay relevant as models evolve — a mental model, not a recipe
- Course is model-agnostic; examples use Claude but principles apply broadly
- Practical exercises require direct LLM interaction throughout

# AI Fluency: The 4D Framework

## Summary
Core lesson introducing the four competencies of AI Fluency: Delegation, Description, Discernment, and Diligence. Explains how they apply across all three collaboration modes (Automation, Augmentation, Agency) and why developing them prepares you for evolving AI capabilities.

## Key takeaways
- The 4Ds apply across all three engagement modes — they are not mode-specific
- Delegation = deciding what work to do with AI vs. yourself
- Description = communicating effectively with AI
- Discernment = evaluating AI outputs critically
- Diligence = ensuring responsible AI collaboration
- The goal is not to automate everything but to create the most effective human-AI partnership

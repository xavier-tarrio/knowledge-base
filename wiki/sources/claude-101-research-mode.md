# Claude 101: Research Mode for Deep Dives

## Summary
Lesson covering Claude's Research mode — an agentic multi-source investigation feature that conducts iterative web searches with extended thinking to produce comprehensive, cited reports. Covers when to use Research vs. web search vs. extended thinking vs. enterprise search, how the research loop works, and tips for writing effective Research prompts.

## Key takeaways
- Research is agentic — Claude autonomously decides what to search next based on what it finds, pursuing leads without you directing each step
- Extended thinking is always active in Research mode — Claude both plans its investigation AND gathers information
- Most reports complete in 5–15 minutes; complex investigations up to 45 minutes — time that would otherwise be hours of manual research
- All claims include citations for easy verification
- Decision framework: Research (comprehensive synthesis) vs. web search (quick facts) vs. extended thinking (deep reasoning, no retrieval needed) vs. enterprise search (internal org knowledge)
- With Google Workspace connected, Research can combine web findings with your emails, calendar, and documents in a single pass
- Turn off web search to do internal-only research across connected tools

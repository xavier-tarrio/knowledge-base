# AI Fluency: Capabilities & Limitations

## Summary
Examines what generative AI can and cannot do effectively today. Covers versatility, conversational awareness, and tool connectivity as strengths; knowledge cutoffs, hallucinations, context window limits, and complex reasoning failures as limitations. Frames effective AI use as combining complementary human and AI strengths.

## Key takeaways
- Capabilities: versatility across tasks, conversational flow, ability to connect with external tools
- Limitations: knowledge cutoffs, hallucinations (factually incorrect outputs), context window constraints, challenges with complex multi-step reasoning
- The field evolves rapidly — today's limitations are tomorrow's solved problems
- Most effective applications combine human judgment and creativity with AI speed and breadth

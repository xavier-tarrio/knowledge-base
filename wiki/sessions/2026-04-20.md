## Key insights
- The **4D Framework** gives vocabulary for diagnosing AI failures — most problems are one of: wrong task (Delegation), bad prompt (Description), unscrutinised output (Discernment), or no accountability (Diligence).
- **Agency mode** is exactly what this wiki is — Claude configured with CLAUDE.md to run autonomously. You're already practising the highest tier of AI collaboration.
- **Git-diff startup** is the right architecture for a growing wiki: read only what changed, not everything.
- **HTTPS > SSH** for this setup: `gh` manages tokens automatically, no key management needed.
